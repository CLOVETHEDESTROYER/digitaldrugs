## App Description

ZenWave is an immersive sound and meditation app built for focused practice, deep relaxation, visualization, and intentional daily rituals.

CREATE YOUR OWN SOUND SESSION

Use the free binaural and pulsed beat generator to shape a session around sleep preparation, meditation, relaxed focus, or creative flow. Choose a brainwave preset, adjust the frequencies and volume, add ocean ambience, and set a timer that fits your routine.

BUILD A DAILY MANIFESTATION PRACTICE

Return to a fresh daily affirmation, begin a short guided visualization, or choose from a library of longer spoken mantra sessions. Premium members can explore intentions for abundance, love, confidence, purpose, career, success, wellbeing, and calm, or create a personalized manifestation ritual.

EXPLORE GUIDED RITUALS

ZenWave Premium includes Gateway-inspired relaxation and visualization sessions, reflective Quantum Code rituals, recorded voice guidance, and binaural sound beds designed to support repeatable meditation practice.

WORK WITH CHAKRA TONES

Explore seven chakra-inspired sound anchors, move through a timed progression, and blend the tones with optional nature ambience. Each chakra includes concise reflective context and a recommended frequency.

TRACK YOUR PRACTICE

Build consistency with session streaks, badges, challenges, an evolving Gateway constellation, optional ritual reminders, and a private local journal. Your reflections and progress remain on your device.

LISTEN YOUR WAY

- Brainwave presets and custom frequency controls
- Timers and background audio playback
- Daily manifestations and recorded mantra sessions
- Gateway-inspired guided rituals
- Reflective Quantum Code rituals
- Chakra tones and timed progression
- Nature sound layers
- Practice streaks, badges, challenges, and constellation progress

- Educational guides for brainwaves, chakras, Gateway-inspired practice, and Quantum Codes

FREE AND PREMIUM ACCESS

The binaural and pulsed beat generator is available free. ZenWave Premium unlocks the broader ritual library, manifestation experiences, chakra sessions, guided voice practices, challenges, and journaling through monthly or annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Customers who purchased the original paid version of ZenWave before version 2.0 retain Legacy Premium access.

ZenWave offers wellness, reflection, and visualization content. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not guarantee any particular outcome.

## App Review Notes

ZenWave 3.0 is a major feature and interface update. No account or login is required.

FREE EXPERIENCE

After onboarding, a user without Premium access is directed to the Waves tab. The complete binaural and pulsed beat generator is free, including brainwave presets, custom frequency controls, timers, playback controls, ocean ambience, and educational information.

PREMIUM EXPERIENCE

Premium content is available from the Home and Manifest tabs. This includes:

- Daily manifestation rituals and six recorded mantra sessions
- The custom manifestation builder
- Gateway-inspired guided relaxation and visualization sessions
- Reflective Quantum Code rituals
- Chakra tones, timed chakra progression, and expanded nature-sound mixing
- Gateway constellation progression, challenges, badges, reminders, and local journaling

The Home tab contains guided ritual paths and the Gateway constellation. The Manifest tab contains the daily manifestation, recorded mantra library, custom manifestation builder, and the horizontally scrolling chakra library.

IN-APP PURCHASES

ZenWave Premium uses these auto-renewable subscription products:

- com.hyperlabsAI.DigitalDrug.premium.monthly
- com.hyperlabsAI.DigitalDrug.premium.annual

The paywall clearly presents monthly and annual access, automatic-renewal information, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links, and Restore Purchases.

Restore Purchases is available from both the paywall and Settings. Manage Subscription is available in Settings.

LEGACY PURCHASERS

Users whose verified App Store original app version is earlier than 2.0 automatically receive Legacy Premium because the original release was a paid download.

TESTING NOTES

- No credentials are required.
- Notification permission is requested only after a completed session and remains optional.
- Journal entries, manifestation intentions, reminders, and practice progress are stored locally on the device.
- Guided narration begins after a short settling period while the sound bed is already playing.
- Audio is configured to continue during tab changes, screen locking, and background playback.
- Gateway, Quantum Code, manifestation, chakra, and astral-projection content is presented as wellness, reflection, meditation, and visualization practice. The app does not claim medical treatment or guaranteed outcomes.
