# Butterchurn Visual Lab

Bundled offline: Butterchurn 2.6.7 (latest stable at integration) and six selected presets from butterchurn-presets 2.4.7. Both npm distributions declare MIT; their original license notices are included alongside the scripts. Preset authors/names are preserved in presets.js and shown in the lab. No presets have been renamed in their original attribution.

Sources: https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn and https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets

These are community MilkDrop visuals, not original ZenWave artwork. The lab does not download code, presets, images or audio at runtime. The native studio supplies only background PCM; mantra narration never enters the visualizer.
